About

Hi! I am a master's student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University.

My interests center around machine learning and embedded systems, and how they can be combined to build intelligent, real-world technologies. I am interested in building intelligent physical systems that can sense, learn, and make decisions in the real world, with applications in areas such as robotics and wearable health technology.

My research and projects have focused on developing applied machine learning and embedded systems that interact with the physical world. This has included:

  • Autonomous Robotics
  • Wearable Health Technology
  • Computational Neuroscience

Before my Master's, I graduated from Duke University with a degree in Biomedical Engineering and a minor in Computer Science. I was advised by Dr. Angel Peterchev, where I worked on computational modeling for brain stimulation and developed machine learning methods for predicting neural activation.
