Biography

Hi! I am a master's student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University. My interests lie at the intersection of robotics, machine learning, autonomous systems, and intelligent control.

I am particularly interested in building learning-based robotic systems that can perceive their environment, make intelligent decisions, and operate autonomously in the real world. My work has involved robot perception, mapping, navigation, reinforcement learning, and computer vision.

I have also worked on machine learning problems in biomedical engineering and computational neuroscience, including neural modeling and decoding human movement from high-density EMG signals.

I enjoy developing systems that bridge machine learning algorithms with real-world hardware, and I am especially interested in robot learning, autonomous robotics, and intelligent embodied systems.
