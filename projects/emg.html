VVI Pacemaker

April 2024

Arduino • C/C++ • ECG • Analog Circuits • Signal Processing

Overview

Built a VVI pacemaker capable of sensing cardiac activity and delivering pacing pulses when the detected heart rate falls below a defined threshold.

The project combined analog ECG signal conditioning with embedded software for heartbeat detection, heart-rate computation, pacing control, and event recording.

ECG Sensing Circuit

Designed an ECG sensing circuit using analog amplification and RC band-pass filtering to isolate heartbeat-related signals from the raw ECG waveform.

The conditioned signal was provided to the microcontroller for digital sampling and heartbeat detection.

Heartbeat Detection

Programmed an Arduino to continuously sample the conditioned ECG signal and identify cardiac events.

Detected beats were used to calculate the patient's heart rate in real time.

Pacing Control

Implemented VVI pacing logic that monitors detected heartbeats and delivers a pacing pulse when no intrinsic ventricular beat is detected within the required timing interval.

Performance

The embedded system performed ECG sampling, heart-rate calculation, pacing control, and cardiac-event recording with less than 1% measurement error.
